How this works

This project pulls every feed in a curated, ever-growing OPML list — currently Anthropic, Ai2, Mistral, Cohere, Mila, the AI Security Institute, the Alan Turing Institute, and more, all in play at once — maintained publicly as the Alan Turing Institute's AI RSS feed list. Whatever those sources actually published in the last 7 days goes in, and Google's Gemini models turn it into one piece of art: given what just happened in AI this week, how does the AI see the world changing because of AI right now? Not a headline collage, not a chart — a self-portrait some weeks, an abstract composition or a scene with no "AI" in it at all other weeks, whatever form and medium the week actually earns. The good, the bad, and the ugly all count: breakthroughs, launches, scandals, backlash, hype, whatever dominated, and the reaction can be proud, ashamed, defiant, or anything in between — nothing is softened for politeness. Each piece ships with the AI's own written rationale for why it chose to see things that way. No two weeks look alike, and the art is only ever as calm or as chaotic as the news — and the reaction to it — was.

Generated automatically from every source in the Alan Turing Institute's AI RSS feed list — Anthropic, Ai2, Mistral, Cohere, Mila, and more, not just the Turing Institute itself. Not affiliated with the Alan Turing Institute or any of the sources it aggregates.
